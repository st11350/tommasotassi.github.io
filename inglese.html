Inglese

L'argomento che mi ha colpito di più.

James Joyce

What struck me was how Joyce manages to take an epic poem like the Odyssey and transform it into an ordinary day in the life of a common man in Dublin. The idea that Odysseus' ten-year journey becomes Leopold Bloom's one-day walk made me realize how much the vision of the hero has changed in the modern age. I was especially interested in the stream-of-consciousness technique, because it is a completely different way of storytelling: thoughts do not follow a logical order, but flow one after another just like real thoughts do: chaotic and unpredictable.

Tommaso Tassi
